Key Mathematical Takeaway: In standard deep learning for derivative pricing, training on price alone produces networks with erratic first and second derivatives. The Sobolev objective incorporates analytical Carr-Madan Jacobians:

L_sobolev = || y_hat - y_true ||^2 + λ_sobolev * || ∇_x y_hat - ∇_x y_true ||^2
